Several agents, one session, one worktree.

close it, update it, they keep going

Quit Capu. Your agents don't notice.

Sessions run in Capu Host, a small background service on each machine, not in the window you're looking at. Close the app, restart for an update or let your laptop sleep while the devbox works. When you come back, every session is right where it is, with nothing lost.
